# Research Group Meetings

Our weekly group meeting is the intellectual heartbeat of our lab, a dedicated and protected time for us to come together.

The goal is to foster a dynamic environment for collaborative learning, critical inquiry, and intellectual growth.

It is a space where we share knowledge, challenge assumptions, and inspire new directions in our research, together.

## Goals

- **To Learn Collectively**: We come from different backgrounds and are at different stages in our careers. We should leverage that diversity and create an environment where we are all simultaneously teachers and learners.

- **To Broaden Our Horizons**: To stay current with the latest advancements within our field and to strategically explore adjacent topics that may inform and enrich our work.

- **To Spark Collaboration**: To identify points of synergy between our individual projects and lay the groundwork for new, collaborative research efforts.

- **To Sharpen Our Skills**: To practice critically analyzing research, formulating insightful questions, and clearly articulating complex ideas.

## Shared Commitment

To make our meetings a success, we all agree to the following principles:

- **Be Present & Punctual**: Your consistent presence and perspective are vital. We commit to attending every week unless traveling for a conference, on vacation, or facing an emergency.

- **Prepare Thoroughly**: The quality of our discussion depends on our collective preparation. **Reading the paper is the minimum requirement; "skimming" is not enough**. Come with notes, questions, and ideas.

- **Engage with Focus**: This is a dedicated time for deep and focused thinking: we agree to minimize distractions. **We encourage a "single-screen" approach for notes or relevant lookups**, and silencing and stowing away mobile phones.

- **Contribute with Respect**: We are here to learn from each other. **We challenge ideas, not people**. We foster an environment free of judgment, where everyone feels empowered to contribute, ask for clarification, and express uncertainty.

## The meeting

- Logistics
    - **When**: Every Tuesday, 11:30 - 13:30
    - **Where**: beta2 @ BCAM

- The meeting is divided into **two distinct (~60 minutes) but flexible parts**.
    
    1. **Part 1: The Presenter's Lead**
        - The designated presenter for the week will guide us through the chosen material.
            - However, **this is not intended to be a one-way lecture**.

        - The presenter retains control of the pace and focus to ensure the key material is covered within the first hour.
            - The presenter's role is to provide context, explain the core concepts, and steer the discussion.

        - Clarifying questions and brief, on-topic discussions are highly encouraged throughout the presentation.

    2. **The Deep Dive**: open, deep and collaborative discussion
        - Solidify Understanding:
            - Ensure everyone grasps the key methods, results, and takeaways.
            - No question is too basic.

        - Identify Gaps: Critically assess the work.
            - What are its limitations?
            - What questions remain unanswered?
            - What knowledge gaps (in our own understanding or in the field) does this work expose?

        - Next Steps: Brainstorm future directions, for research and for us.
            - How does this connect to our ongoing projects?
            - What is the logical next experiment?
            - What should we read next to follow this thread?

- At some point, it's ok to move from "what the paper says" to "what it means for us."
